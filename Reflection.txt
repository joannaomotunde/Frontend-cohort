Research and Reflection
I use websites and web applications like LinkedIn, YouTube, Google Docs, and WPS Office almost every day. Among these, I find LinkedIn to be one of the best-designed platforms. It is very well-organized and easy to navigate. The layout is clean, the icons are simple to understand, and everything feels nice. Whether I’m updating my profile, checking job opportunities, or connecting with people, the process is smooth. The notifications, message layout, and overall flow make it easy to stay active and engaged without feeling overwhelmed.
By the end of this cohort, I hope to build my own personal portfolio website where I can showcase my projects and growth as a front-end developer. I’d also love to learn how to create simple, interactive websites that look good and function well.
I’m really excited about learning how to bring designs to life using HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, especially seeing how small lines of code can change how a website looks or behaves. What makes me a bit nervous is understanding JavaScript and debugging, because it feels a little more technical. But I’m ready to take it one step at a time and keep practicing until it starts to make sense.
